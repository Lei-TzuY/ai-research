# Research Validity Policy

`ai-research` treats evidence status as part of the implementation contract.

- **Hypothesis**: proposed mechanism or relationship; not yet demonstrated.
- **Deterministic verification**: a reproducible executable check over a bounded artifact/workload.
- **Empirical result**: an observation produced by a pre-specified study protocol and retained evidence.
- **Generalization/effectiveness claim**: requires evidence beyond a deterministic MWE and must be justified by the corresponding study design.

A later umbrella migration or integration must preserve the status attached to existing studies and HOLD decisions. It may add new evidence, but it may not retroactively relabel earlier evidence after outcomes are known.
